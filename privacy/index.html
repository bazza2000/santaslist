Santa's List – Privacy Policy

Applies to Santa's List and Santa's List Free for Android. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Santa's List is a family game in which a parent enters a child's details and the child "scans" their thumb to see whether they are on Santa's Good List or Naughty List.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

Information you enter

The names, ages, wished-for toys, list choices and optional photos you add are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Photos are chosen with Android's photo picker, which gives the app access only to the photos you select. A copy of each chosen photo is kept in the app's storage.

Deleting your information

You can delete a child (and their photo) at any time from the app's edit screen. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is designed for families with young children. Because it collects no data, it collects no information from children.
